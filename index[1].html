frame 01

You show up. Not once, not when it’s easy — every single day, like it’s the most normal thing in the world. It isn’t. Most people quit long before here.

frame 02

Every morning, the same road — one town to another, before most people are even awake. Anyone else would call that a reason to slow down. You just call it the warm-up.

frame 03

The gym at dawn. The oats at breakfast. The quiet way you look after yourself — no audience, no applause. That kind of discipline is the one thing nobody can fake.

frame 04

Some things you do are quietly unfair. The way you say “okay” like you’ve already won the argument. The way you look after yourself like it costs you nothing. You probably have no idea how distracting that is — that’s the unfair part.

frame 05

So I made this. With my own hands, line by line. Not a confession, not a hint — just something built properly, for someone I genuinely respect.

final frame

Some people are worth making something for, even if they never ask why.

This is that. No reply needed — no score to settle, no debt. Just a small piece of art from someone who noticed how hard you work, Sharmin — and thought you deserved to see it.
